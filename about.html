ABOUT PAKISTAN FOOD

Pakistani recipes made clearer for everyday home cooking

Pakistan Food is a recipe website focused on familiar Pakistani dishes, regional favourites, snacks, desserts and drinks. Our goal is to make each recipe easy to follow, useful on mobile, and accessible in both English and Urdu.

Clear methodsRecipes are structured with measured ingredients, step-by-step instructions and practical cooking notes.
English + UrduKey recipe content is presented bilingually so more home cooks can use the site comfortably.
Transparent updatesRecipes may be corrected or improved when an ingredient, method, image or technical detail needs clarification.
Permanent recipe pagesEach recipe has its own stable URL with category links and related recipes for easier browsing.

How our recipe content is prepared

We write and edit recipe instructions in original wording around established Pakistani dishes and common home-cooking techniques. We review ingredient lists against the method, improve unclear steps, add serving and storage guidance where useful, and correct broken links or technical issues when they are found.

Cooking times, serving sizes and ingredient quantities are practical guides rather than guarantees because ingredients, heat levels and kitchen equipment can vary. Home cooks should always check texture and doneness before serving.

Accuracy and food safety

We avoid inventing reader ratings, nutrition values or professional credentials. Food-safety notes are added where they materially help, and readers with allergies or medical dietary requirements should verify ingredients and seek appropriate professional advice when needed. See our Disclaimer for more detail.

Images and attribution

Pakistan Food uses a mix of local site images, generated recipe illustrations and appropriately sourced external images. Where attribution is required, source and licensing information is maintained on the Image Credits page. Illustrative images are not presented as proof that a specific recipe was photographed after testing.

Editorial independence

Advertising may appear on Pakistan Food, but advertising does not determine which recipes are included or how cooking instructions are written. Our Editorial Policy explains the standards used for recipe content and corrections.

Contact and corrections

If you notice a missing ingredient, unclear instruction, broken link or another issue, please contact us at manazarali01@gmail.com. Useful corrections help us improve the site for future readers.
