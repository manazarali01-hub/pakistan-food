Privacy Policy

Pakistan Food respects the privacy of its visitors. This page explains the types of information that may be processed when you use this website and how third-party services may be involved.

Information you provide

If you contact us by email, the information you choose to include in your message is handled through your email provider. Pakistan Food does not use the website contact form to store your message on the site.

Cookies and advertising

Pakistan Food uses Google AdSense or may use it when advertising is enabled. Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies or similar technologies to serve ads based on a visitor's prior visits to this website or to other websites.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables Google and its partners to serve ads that may be personalized according to a user's visits to this and other websites. Visitors can manage or opt out of personalized advertising through Google's My Ad Center. Information about how Google uses data from sites that use its services is available at Google's partner-sites privacy information.

Other third-party advertising vendors or ad networks may also use cookies where applicable. Visitors can review the privacy and opt-out choices offered by those providers through their own websites and privacy controls.

Consent and regional privacy choices

Where required by applicable law or Google policy, a consent message may be shown before eligible advertising technologies are used. Visitors in regions with additional privacy requirements may be offered controls for consent, ad personalization and related data processing.

Third-party services

Pages may load resources from third-party services, including Google services and image delivery sources. Those providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser information, device information, and request data according to their own privacy policies.

Your choices

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Where a consent message is shown, you can use its controls to manage eligible advertising and privacy choices. You can also use Google's advertising controls linked above to manage personalized advertising.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website, advertising setup, or applicable requirements change.
