Editorial Policy

Pakistan Food aims to publish clear, useful Pakistani recipe content for everyday home cooking while being transparent about how the material is prepared and maintained.

Recipe content

Recipes are written and edited in original wording around familiar Pakistani dishes and practical home-cooking methods. Ingredient quantities, cooking times and serving sizes are intended as usable guides; kitchens, ingredients and equipment vary, so cooks should use judgement and check doneness before serving.

Accuracy and updates

We review recipes for missing ingredients, unclear steps, broken links and technical errors. When a recipe needs correction or a clearer method, the page may be updated without changing its permanent URL.

Ratings, nutrition and claims

Pakistan Food does not invent reader ratings, reviews, nutrition values or professional credentials. Such information is only shown when there is a genuine basis for it.

Images and attribution

Recipe photography may use local site assets or appropriately sourced external images. Where attribution is required, source information is maintained on the Image Credits page.

Advertising and editorial independence

Advertising may appear on the website, but ad placement does not determine which recipes are included or how cooking instructions are written.

Corrections

If you notice an error or have a useful correction, contact us at manazarali01@gmail.com.
