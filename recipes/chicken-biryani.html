Chicken Biryani Recipe

Authentic Pakistani chicken biryani made with aromatic basmati rice, tender chicken, yogurt, fried onions and traditional spices.

Cooking time: 60 min · Serves: 6 · Category: Rice

Ingredients

  • 750 g bone-in chicken
  • 3 cups basmati rice
  • 3 onions, thinly sliced
  • 3 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 cup plain yogurt
  • 2 tbsp biryani masala
  • 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 4 green chilies
  • ½ cup mint and coriander
  • Salt and cooking oil

How to Make Chicken Biryani

  1. Wash and soak the basmati rice.
  2. Cook chicken with onions, tomatoes, yogurt and spices.
  3. Boil rice separately until nearly cooked.
  4. Layer rice and chicken masala.
  5. Cover and cook on low heat for 15–20 minutes.
