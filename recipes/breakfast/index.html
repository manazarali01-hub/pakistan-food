--- layout: category category_name: Breakfast category_slug: breakfast meta_description: Pakistani breakfast recipes such as aloo paratha, halwa puri and anda paratha, with practical dough, filling and pan-cooking tips for home kitchens. intro: Build a Pakistani breakfast around the time you have: make anda paratha for a quick egg-and-bread meal, aloo paratha for a filling potato-stuffed flatbread, or halwa puri with chickpeas when you want a fuller weekend-style spread. For soft parathas, keep dough covered while it rests, cool cooked potato filling before stuffing, and seal the edges without trapping air. Cook over medium heat so the centre warms before the outside browns; for puri, use properly heated oil and fry close to serving. Prepare chutney, yogurt or chai while the breads cook, and open each recipe for measured ingredients, timings and the complete method. ---
