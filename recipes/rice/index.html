--- layout: category category_name: Rice category_slug: rice meta_description: Pakistani rice recipes for chicken biryani, beef pulao and daal chawal, with practical basmati, parboiling and dum tips. intro: Choose chicken biryani when you want spiced chicken and partly cooked basmati layered for a final dum; choose beef pulao for rice cooked in a savoury stock with the meat and whole spices; or make daal chawal for a simpler everyday meal. For biryani, rinse and soak basmati as its variety requires, then drain it while the centre is still firm so it can finish steaming without turning mushy. For pulao, season the cooking liquid before adding rice and adjust the liquid to your rice and pot rather than relying on one ratio for every variety.
