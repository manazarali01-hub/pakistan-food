Beef Nihari Recipe

Traditional Pakistani beef nihari, slowly cooked with deep spices until the beef is tender and the gravy is rich.

Cooking time: 4 hrs · Serves: 6 · Category: Beef

Ingredients

  • 1 kg beef shank
  • 3 tbsp nihari masala
  • 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • ⅓ cup wheat flour
  • ½ cup cooking oil
  • 7 cups water
  • Fresh ginger and coriander
  • Green chilies, lemon and salt

How to Make Beef Nihari

  1. Fry beef with ginger garlic.
  2. Add nihari spices.
  3. Add plenty of water.
  4. Slow cook until beef is tender.
  5. Add flour slurry to thicken.
