How to use this Pakistani recipe directory

This page is the complete recipe index, separate from the Pakistan Food homepage. Start with a category when you know the kind of dish you want, or use the popular links below when you are choosing a familiar Pakistani meal.

Rice, biryani and pulao

For layered rice, yakhni-style pulao and everyday rice dishes, begin with Pakistani rice recipes. You can compare chicken biryani, Sindhi biryani, mutton biryani, chicken pulao, matar pulao and Kabli pulao from one focused category.

Chicken, beef and slow-cooked dishes

Use the chicken and beef directories for karahi, qorma, handi, nihari, haleem, paya and keema-based dishes without mixing them into snack or dessert results.

BBQ, kababs and snacks

The BBQ collection covers tikka and kabab-style cooking, while snacks groups samosas, pakoras, chaat and other smaller dishes that suit tea time or casual serving.

Breakfast, sweets and drinks

Open breakfast, desserts or drinks when you want halwa puri, paratha, kheer, gulab jamun, lassi, chai or sharbat instead of a main-course recipe.
