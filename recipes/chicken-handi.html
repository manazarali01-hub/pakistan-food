Chicken Handi Recipe

Creamy Pakistani chicken handi made with tender boneless chicken, yogurt, tomatoes, cream and aromatic spices.

Cooking time: 45 min · Serves: 4 · Category: Chicken

Ingredients

  • 750 g boneless chicken
  • ½ cup cooking cream
  • ½ cup plain yogurt
  • 4 tomatoes, blended
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 1½ tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • Salt and cooking oil

How to Make Chicken Handi

  1. Cook onions and chicken.
  2. Add tomatoes and spices.
  3. Add yogurt and cook well.
  4. Add cream.
  5. Simmer for 10 minutes.
