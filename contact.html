Contact & Recipe Corrections

Found something that could make a recipe clearer or more accurate? Please tell us. Useful corrections are reviewed against the ingredient list, method and related page before the content is updated.

What you can report

  • An ingredient that is missing from the method, or a method step that mentions an ingredient not listed.
  • A quantity, timing or instruction that is unclear or appears inconsistent.
  • A broken recipe, guide, category, source or affiliate link.
  • An image that does not accurately represent the named dish or has an attribution problem.
  • A mobile, navigation, printing, language-switching or accessibility problem.
  • A factual or wording correction that would make the page more useful to a home cook.

What to include

Please include the page URL, the section or step involved, what appears wrong, and—when useful—the correction you suggest. Screenshots can help with layout or image problems.

How corrections are handled

We do not promise that every suggestion will be adopted. When a correction is supported and improves the page, the recipe or guide may be edited while keeping its permanent URL where practical. We avoid creating fake reader reviews, ratings or endorsements from correction emails.

Advertising and commercial questions

Advertising or affiliate relationships do not determine recipe instructions or correction decisions. See the Editorial Policy and Image Credits for more detail.
