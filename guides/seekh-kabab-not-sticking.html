How to Stop Seekh Kabab from Falling Off the Skewer

Quick answer: Seekh kabab mixture holds better when the mince is not watery, ingredients are finely prepared, the mixture is well combined and it is chilled before shaping.

Control moisture

Drain excess liquid from onions or other wet additions. A loose mixture struggles to grip the skewer.

Mix until cohesive

Work the mince and seasonings until the mixture becomes slightly tacky and cohesive, then chill it before shaping.

Shape with even pressure

Press a thin, even layer firmly around the skewer. Very thick kababs are heavier and can slip before the outside sets.
