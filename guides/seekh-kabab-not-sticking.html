How to Stop Seekh Kabab from Falling Off the Skewer

Quick answer: Seekh kabab mixture holds better when the mince is not watery, ingredients are finely prepared, the mixture is well combined and it is chilled before shaping.

Remove excess moisture from the mixture

Watery onion, herbs or other additions can loosen the mince and make it slide from the skewer. Finely prepare wet ingredients and squeeze or drain excess liquid when the recipe calls for it.

Mix until the mince becomes cohesive

Seekh kabab mixture needs more cohesion than a loose burger-style mince. Work the meat and seasonings until the mixture becomes slightly tacky and holds together when pressed.

Chill before shaping

A short rest in the refrigerator firms the mixture and lets flavours settle. Very warm mince is softer and more difficult to press securely around a skewer.

Shape a thin, even layer

Press the mixture firmly along the skewer with even thickness. Oversized kababs are heavier and can pull away before the exterior has set.

Start on properly heated equipment

The surface needs enough heat to begin setting the meat promptly. Weak initial heat can leave the mixture soft for longer, while extreme heat can burn the outside before the centre cooks.

If the mixture is still too loose

First correct excess moisture and chilling rather than adding large amounts of dry binder, which can make kababs dense. Follow the specific recipe's binder guidance only as needed.
