Why Jalebi Turns Soft: Common Causes and How to Keep It Crisp

Quick answer: Jalebi can soften when the batter, frying temperature or sugar syrup is out of balance, or when hot jalebi sits in syrup too long and absorbs excessive moisture.

What matters most

Fry until the spirals are properly crisp before syruping. Oil that is too cool encourages oil absorption; excessive heat can colour the outside before the structure crisps.

Technique and common mistakes

The syrup should coat rather than waterlog the jalebi. A very thin syrup or prolonged soaking can quickly soften the fried spirals.

Practical tip

Serve fresh for the best texture. If making ahead, keep exposure to moisture low and avoid sealing warm jalebi in a container where condensation forms.
