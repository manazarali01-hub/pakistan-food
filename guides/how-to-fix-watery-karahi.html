How to Fix Watery Chicken Karahi Without Ruining the Masala

Quick answer: If chicken karahi is watery, cook it uncovered over medium to medium-high heat while stirring regularly so excess moisture evaporates and the masala thickens.

First identify where the extra water came from

Chicken and tomatoes both release moisture as they cook. Added water, a covered pan or very juicy tomatoes can leave more liquid than expected, so the solution begins with stopping unnecessary additions.

Finish uncovered

Once the chicken is safely cooked, remove the lid and let steam escape. A wide karahi or pan gives the liquid more surface area and speeds reduction without requiring a starch thickener.

Use moderately stronger heat with attention

Increase heat enough to evaporate excess liquid, but stir and scrape frequently. As the gravy concentrates, tomato and spice solids can catch on the base much faster.

Do not add flour just because the gravy is thin

Flour or cornflour changes the texture of the masala. Karahi normally gets its body from reduced tomatoes and cooking juices, so evaporation is the more natural correction.

Protect the chicken from overcooking

If the chicken is already very tender but a large amount of liquid remains, reduce efficiently in a wide pan rather than simmering gently for a very long time. Extended cooking can dry the meat.

Know when reduction is complete

Stop when the masala clings to the chicken and excess watery liquid is gone. Some visible cooking fat around the concentrated masala can be normal; continuing far beyond this point risks scorching.

Urdu / Roman Urdu: Karahi mein pani zyada ho jaye to kya karein?

Roman Urdu: Karahi watery ho to pehle lid hata kar controlled heat par extra moisture reduce karein. Masalay ko stir karte rahen taa ke neeche na lage; sirf gravy gaadhi dikhane ke liye unnecessary flour ya starch add na karein.
