How to Fix Watery Chicken Karahi Without Ruining the Masala

Quick answer: If chicken karahi is watery, cook it uncovered over medium to medium-high heat while stirring regularly so excess moisture evaporates and the masala thickens.

What matters most

Do not immediately add flour or starch; traditional karahi texture is better developed by reduction. Keep the pan uncovered and watch closely as the liquid decreases.

Technique and common mistakes

Tomatoes and chicken both release water. Adding extra water early can leave the finished masala thin. Use only what the recipe needs and let tomatoes cook down properly.

Practical tip

Once the masala clings to the chicken and oil begins to separate around the edges, reduce the heat. Over-reducing can dry the chicken and scorch the spices.
