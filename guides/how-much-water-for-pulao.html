How Much Water Do You Need for Pakistani Pulao?

Quick answer: For absorption-style pulao, the exact liquid depends on rice age and soaking, but soaked basmati commonly needs less liquid than unsoaked rice. Start from your rice brand's guidance and adjust from experience.

Why one ratio does not fit every rice

Older basmati, fresh rice and different brands absorb differently. Soaking also changes how much liquid the rice needs in the pot.

Count the stock already present

Meat stock, tomato moisture and any liquid left in the masala all contribute. Measure the total cooking liquid rather than adding water blindly.

Adjust in small steps

If rice is consistently hard, add slightly more liquid next time. If it is soft or sticky, reduce liquid or shorten soaking.
