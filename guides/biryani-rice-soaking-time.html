Biryani Rice Soaking Time: How Long Should Basmati Rice Soak?

Quick answer: For most basmati biryani, soak rinsed rice for about 20–30 minutes. Older, extra-long basmati may benefit from roughly 30 minutes; very long soaking can make grains fragile.

What matters most

Rinse basmati until the water is much clearer, then soak it in fresh water. The goal is even hydration so the grain cooks more uniformly without needing excessive boiling.

Technique and common mistakes

Drain the soaked rice well before adding it to boiling salted water. For layered biryani, stop boiling while the grain still has a firm centre because it continues cooking during dum.

Practical tip

If your rice turns mushy, shorten either the soak or the boil. If the centre stays hard after dum, slightly increase pre-cooking next time.
