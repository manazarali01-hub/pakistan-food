Chicken Qorma vs Chicken Handi: What Is the Difference?

Quick answer: Pakistani qorma usually builds depth from browned onions, yogurt and warm spices, while handi is commonly creamier with a softer restaurant-style gravy.

How qorma gets flavour

Browning onions well is central to many qorma recipes. Yogurt and spices then create a fragrant gravy with a deeper profile.

What makes handi different

Handi recipes often emphasize a creamy, smooth gravy. Tomato, cream or yogurt may play a stronger role depending on the version.

Which should you cook?

Choose qorma for an aromatic onion-and-yogurt curry; choose handi for a softer, creamier gravy. Both pair well with naan or rice.
