How to Reheat Biryani Without Drying It Out

Quick answer: Reheat biryani covered with a small splash of water so steam restores moisture; heat only the portion you need until it is piping hot throughout.

Add moisture sparingly

A little water creates steam, but too much can make the rice soggy. Sprinkle rather than pour.

Use gentle covered heat

A covered pan over low heat works well for larger portions. Stir as little as possible to protect the grains.

Reheat only what you need

Repeated cooling and reheating hurts quality and food safety. Portion leftovers before reheating.
