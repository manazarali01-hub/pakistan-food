How to Cook Daal Without Making It Too Mushy

Quick answer: Choose the right lentil, rinse it, use controlled water and check it near the end of cooking; stop when the grains reach the texture your dish needs.

Different lentils cook differently

Masoor, moong, mash and chana dal do not soften at the same rate. Follow timing for the specific lentil rather than one universal rule.

Add water carefully

Too much water can turn a thick daal into soup. Start with a sensible amount and add hot water only when needed.

Finish with tarka

Once the lentils are cooked, a fresh tarka adds aroma without requiring more long cooking that could break down the daal further.
