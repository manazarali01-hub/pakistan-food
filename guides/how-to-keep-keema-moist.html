How to Keep Keema Moist, Not Dry

Quick answer: Brown minced meat for flavour, but keep enough masala and moisture in the pan and stop cooking once the meat is tender instead of reducing it until dry.

Build the masala first

Cook onions and tomatoes until the raw edge is gone before adding or finishing the mince. A well-cooked masala coats the meat and helps it stay succulent.

Control evaporation

Use a lid when tenderness needs time, then uncover only long enough to reach the texture you want. A wide pan will dry keema faster.

Add vegetables at the right time

For aloo keema or keema matar, account for the water released or absorbed by the vegetables. Add small amounts of hot water only when needed.
