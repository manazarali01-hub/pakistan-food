Best Meat for Nihari: Cuts That Stay Tender in Slow Cooking

Quick answer: Nihari benefits from beef or mutton cuts with connective tissue that soften during slow cooking. Shank is a classic choice because long cooking develops a rich broth and tender meat.

What matters most

Lean, quick-cooking cuts can dry out before the gravy develops enough body. Bone-in shank can add depth, while boneless pieces are convenient if you prefer easier serving.

Technique and common mistakes

Cook gently rather than boiling aggressively. Slow heat gives collagen time to soften and helps the meat remain intact instead of becoming stringy.

Practical tip

Exact cooking time depends on the cut, piece size and whether you use a pot or pressure cooker, so judge doneness by tenderness rather than the clock alone.
