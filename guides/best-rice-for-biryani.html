Best Rice for Biryani: Choosing Basmati for Separate, Long Grains

Quick answer: Aged long-grain basmati is a dependable choice for biryani because it is aromatic and tends to stay separate when rinsed, soaked and cooked carefully.

What matters most

Look for basmati labelled aged or extra-long grain. Grain quality matters, but technique matters too: rinse away loose surface starch, soak briefly and avoid overcooking before dum.

Technique and common mistakes

If basmati is unavailable, another good-quality long-grain rice can work, but texture and aroma will differ. Adjust water and cooking time to the rice on the package.

Practical tip

For a first attempt, use one rice consistently so you can learn its soaking and parboiling time instead of changing brands every cook.
