How to Balance Sweetness and Tang in Pakistani Sharbat

Quick answer: Start with less syrup or sugar than you think you need, dilute with cold water, add acidity gradually, then taste again over ice before making the final adjustment.

Dilute first

Concentrated syrup tastes different before dilution. Mix the base with most of the water before deciding whether more sweetness is needed.

Add sourness gradually

Lemon, tamarind or dried plum can quickly dominate. Add in small amounts, stir thoroughly and taste between additions.

Taste at serving temperature

Very cold drinks can taste less sweet. Make the final adjustment with the sharbat chilled or poured over ice.
