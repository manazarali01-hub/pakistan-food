Pakistani Rice Dishes: Biryani, Pulao and More

Quick answer: Pakistani rice cooking ranges from layered biryani to stock-cooked pulao and simpler everyday rice meals; the key differences are seasoning, liquid and assembly.

Biryani is layered

Biryani usually cooks rice and masala partly separately before layering and finishing on dum, creating distinct colours and flavours.

Pulao cooks together

Pulao typically cooks rice in seasoned stock so flavour is absorbed more evenly through the grains.

Choose by occasion

Biryani suits a bold centrepiece, while pulao is often gentler and easier to pair with curries, kababs or raita.
