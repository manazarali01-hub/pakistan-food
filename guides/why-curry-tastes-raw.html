Why Does Curry Taste Raw?

Quick answer: “Raw” can mean several different flavours. Sharp onion, pungent ginger-garlic, acidic tomato, uncooked yogurt or dusty powdered spice each needs a different correction. Taste and smell the gravy before adding more masala.

Raw onion tastes sharp and sulphurous

If onion was only softened when the recipe needed golden browning, the sauce can taste harsh and thin. Continue cooking the onion base with enough fat and moderate heat until its raw smell is gone. For qorma, properly browned fried onion is especially important.

Raw ginger-garlic is pungent

Fresh paste needs brief cooking in fat before a large amount of liquid is added. If it goes straight into water, its sharpness can remain suspended in the gravy for much longer. Cook until the aggressively raw smell softens, but do not burn the garlic.

Raw tomato tastes bright and watery

Tomatoes need time to collapse and lose excess water. In karahi, the masala should reduce until it clings to the chicken. A red sauce can still be undercooked even when the chicken itself is done.

Powdered spices can taste dusty

Chilli, coriander and turmeric should be cooked with moisture and fat long enough to lose their dry powder character. If the pan is nearly dry, add a spoonful of water rather than scorching the spices.

Yogurt needs controlled cooking

Whisk yogurt before adding, lower the heat and stir it in gradually. Then cook the masala until the raw sour dairy note softens and the sauce becomes cohesive. Very high heat can split it before the flavour develops.

If the meat is already tender

Do not punish the meat with another long simmer. If possible, lift cooked chicken pieces out, finish the sauce in a wide pan, then return them briefly to heat through.

Roman Urdu

Raw curry ka reason identify karein: onion, ginger-garlic, tomato, spices ya yogurt. Har cheez ka fix alag hai. Chicken already tender ho to meat ko overcook karne ke bajaye sauce ko separately reduce karke pieces wapas add karein.
