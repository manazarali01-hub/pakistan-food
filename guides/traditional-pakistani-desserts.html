Traditional Pakistani Desserts: Kheer, Gulab Jamun, Jalebi and More

Quick answer: Pakistani dessert tables include milk-based puddings, syrup-soaked sweets and festive favourites. Kheer, gulab jamun, jalebi, sheer khurma and gajar ka halwa each use a different technique.

What matters most

Rice kheer thickens milk slowly with rice; gulab jamun relies on soft fried dumplings absorbing syrup; jalebi combines a crisp fried spiral with syrup; sheer khurma is associated especially with Eid celebrations.

Technique and common mistakes

Choose dessert by occasion and preparation time. Kheer can be chilled ahead, while jalebi is at its best when fresh and crisp.

Practical tip

If you are learning, start with one dessert and master its texture before preparing a large mixed sweet table.
