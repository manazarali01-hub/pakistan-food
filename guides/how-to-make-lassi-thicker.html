Lassi Too Thin? How to Make Lassi Thicker

Quick answer: Start with thick chilled yogurt and add water or milk gradually. If lassi is already too thin, blend in more thick yogurt rather than trying to hide the problem with extra sugar. Remember that melting ice and juicy fruit can thin it further.

Start with the yogurt

Yogurt is the main source of body in traditional lassi. Naturally thick yogurt produces a fuller drink with less adjustment, while loose yogurt needs less added liquid. Stir the yogurt first so you can judge its starting consistency before measuring water or milk.

Add liquid gradually

Recipes give useful starting quantities, but yogurt brands and homemade batches vary. Add part of the water or milk, blend, then check the pour. It is much easier to loosen a thick lassi than to recover one after too much liquid has been added.

Ice can make lassi watery

Ice chills the drink quickly, but it also melts. If you want a thick lassi, use chilled ingredients and add only the amount of ice you actually need. A lassi that sits for a while with a large amount of ice can become noticeably thinner.

Mango and other fruit change the texture

Fruit is not just flavour: ripe mango can add substantial body, while very juicy fruit can introduce more free liquid. For mango lassi, adjust the added milk or water after considering how thick the fruit pulp already is.

How to fix lassi that is already too thin

Add thick yogurt a little at a time and blend briefly until the drink reaches the consistency you want. If the flavour becomes less sweet or less salty after adding yogurt, correct the seasoning afterward. Avoid adding dry thickeners unless a particular recipe is designed for them.

Do not over-correct a thick lassi

Lassi should still be drinkable. If it becomes too thick, loosen it with a small amount of cold water or milk, blend briefly and reassess. The ideal texture depends on whether you are making sweet, salted or fruit lassi, so use the final drinking texture as your guide.
