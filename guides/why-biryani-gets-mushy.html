Why Does Biryani Get Mushy?

Quick answer: Biryani usually turns mushy when the rice is cooked too far before layering, the masala contains too much liquid, or the assembled pot receives more steam and heat than the grains can handle.

The rice was overcooked before layering

Biryani rice has to finish cooking during dum, so it should still have a firm centre when it is drained. Rice that is already completely tender before layering has very little room for the final steaming stage and can soften until the grains collapse.

The masala was too watery

Chicken, meat, tomatoes and yogurt all release moisture. If the masala is still loose when the rice is layered, that extra liquid rises as steam and continues hydrating the grains. Reduce the masala until it is concentrated and coats the meat rather than leaving a large pool of thin gravy.

The rice carried extra water into the pot

Drain parboiled rice promptly and thoroughly. Water trapped with the rice becomes additional steam during dum. At the same time, avoid leaving drained rice sitting so long that the grains become cold, dry or fragile before assembly.

Too much stirring broke the grains

Cooked basmati is delicate. Vigorous mixing releases starch and breaks long grains, which makes the finished biryani feel softer and stickier. Layer with a light hand and, after resting, lift portions gently from the side instead of repeatedly stirring the whole pot.

Dum was too hot or too long

Dum needs gentle trapped heat, not aggressive boiling. Once most of the cooking is complete, very high heat can scorch the bottom while excess steam continues softening the upper rice. Use low heat and judge the finish by the rice, aroma and remaining moisture rather than extending the time automatically.

How to prevent mushy biryani next time

Rinse the rice well, soak it only as appropriate for the variety, parboil it with a firm centre, drain thoroughly and layer it over a properly reduced masala. Finish on controlled low heat, then rest the covered pot briefly before serving so the steam settles and the grains are easier to lift intact.

Urdu / Roman Urdu: Biryani ke chawal mushy kyun ho jate hain?

Roman Urdu: Biryani aksar tab mushy hoti hai jab rice layering se pehle zyada pak jaye, masala watery ho, chawal extra pani carry kar rahe hon ya dum bohat lamba ya tez ho. Rice aur masala dono ki moisture control karein.
