How to Build Flavorful Yakhni for Pulao

Quick answer: Build yakhni with meat, aromatics and whole spices, then strain and season it for the rice.

Start with meat and cold water

This is the first practical factor to control. It sets up the ingredient for the rest of the method and prevents avoidable texture problems.

Choose whole spices for a clean broth

Handle this stage gently and consistently. Basmati and pulao techniques reward control more than aggressive mixing or extreme heat.

Add onion and aromatics for depth

Use the food itself as a cue: look at grain firmness, moisture, aroma and visible steam instead of relying only on a timer.

Simmer gently rather than violently

Avoid compensating with extra water or cooking time unless the rice actually needs it. Those corrections can quickly create softness or dilution.

Strain without pressing cloudy solids

Cookware, rice age and stove strength vary, so the same principle may need a small timing adjustment in different kitchens.

Season the yakhni before measuring for rice

Once this stage is correct, continue with minimal handling. That protects long grains and makes the result easier to repeat.
