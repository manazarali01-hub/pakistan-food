How to Keep Samosas Crispy: Dough, Frying and Storage Tips

Quick answer: For crisp samosas, make a firm dough, avoid an overly wet filling, seal the pastry well and fry at a controlled moderate temperature rather than dropping them into very hot oil.

What matters most

A wet filling steams the pastry from inside. Let cooked potato filling cool before wrapping, and avoid trapping excess moisture.

Technique and common mistakes

Frying too hot can brown the shell before it becomes crisp. Fry in batches so the oil temperature does not swing dramatically and drain on a rack where possible.

Practical tip

For storage, cool samosas before covering them. Trapped steam softens the crust. Reheat in an oven or air fryer for better crispness than a microwave.
