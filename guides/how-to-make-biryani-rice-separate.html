How to Keep Biryani Rice Separate and Fluffy

Quick answer: Separate biryani grains come from rinsing well, controlled soaking, plenty of boiling water and stopping the rice before it is fully cooked.

Rinse and soak with control

Wash basmati gently until the water becomes much clearer. Soak only long enough to hydrate the grain; over-soaked rice becomes delicate and is easier to break.

Parboil, do not fully cook

Boil in plenty of salted water and drain while the grain still has a firm centre. The final steam during dum finishes the cooking.

Handle gently after draining

Do not stir aggressively after draining or during layering. A wide pot and gentle folding help preserve long grains.
