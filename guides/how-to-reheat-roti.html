How to Reheat Roti Without Making It Hard

Quick answer: Use brief gentle heat and avoid prolonged dry reheating.

Use a hot tawa for a brief reheat

This step has the strongest effect on the final result, so keep it deliberate rather than rushing it.

Warm each side only as needed

Small, even distribution works better than concentrating one ingredient in a single area.

Avoid prolonged dry heat

Protect the rice or bread structure while it is hot; unnecessary handling can undo otherwise good cooking.

Cover after reheating

Use aroma, visible moisture and texture to decide when to continue rather than extending the process automatically.

A tiny touch of fat is optional for paratha-style breads

If a correction is needed, make it gradually so flavour and texture stay balanced.

Serve promptly after warming

Finish with a short rest or prompt serving as appropriate; the food continues changing after direct heat stops.

Roman Urdu: Roti dobara soft kaise garam karein?
