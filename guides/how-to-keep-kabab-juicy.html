How to Keep Pakistani Kababs Juicy

Quick answer: Use mince with enough fat, avoid overworking or overcooking it, and cook over properly heated equipment so the outside browns before the inside dries.

Fat supports moisture

Very lean mince can produce dry kababs. A moderate amount of fat helps the mixture stay succulent during high-heat cooking.

Do not overcook

Small kababs cook quickly. Remove them when safely cooked through rather than leaving them over heat just to create more colour.

Rest briefly

A short rest after cooking lets juices settle. Serve soon afterwards because kababs lose moisture as they sit uncovered.
