How to Make Kashmiri Chai Pink

Quick answer: Kashmiri chai develops its pink colour through tea, a small amount of baking soda, aeration and finally milk; the concentrate must first reach a deep reddish tone.

Build the tea concentrate

Simmer the tea with water and a very small amount of baking soda. The concentrate changes colour as it cooks and oxidizes.

Aeration matters

Traditional preparation repeatedly aerates the tea. This helps develop the characteristic colour before milk is added.

Add milk at the right stage

Milk turns the developed reddish concentrate pink. Adding milk too early can leave the drink dull or brownish.
