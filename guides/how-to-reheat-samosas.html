Best Way to Reheat Samosas and Keep Them Crisp

Quick answer: Reheat samosas with dry circulating heat, such as an oven or air fryer, rather than relying on a microwave if crisp pastry is the priority.

Why microwaves soften pastry

Microwaves heat moisture quickly, which can make a fried wrapper soft instead of crisp.

Use moderate dry heat

Warm samosas until the centre is hot and the exterior has crisped again. Avoid excessive heat that browns the shell before the filling warms.

Store before reheating

Cool leftover samosas before refrigerating them in a covered container. Reheat only the amount you plan to eat.
