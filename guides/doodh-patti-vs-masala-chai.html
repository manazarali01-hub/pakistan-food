Doodh Patti vs Masala Chai: What Is the Difference?

Quick answer: Doodh patti is a milk-forward tea made by simmering tea with milk and sugar, while masala chai adds a more pronounced blend of spices such as cardamom, ginger or cinnamon.

What makes doodh patti distinct

Its identity comes from strong tea brewed directly with plenty of milk, producing a rich colour and body.

What makes masala chai distinct

Masala chai emphasizes aromatic spices. The exact blend varies by household and can be kept simple or made more complex.

Avoid bitter tea

Do not boil tea leaves aggressively for too long. Adjust leaf quantity and simmering time rather than trying to fix bitterness with extra sugar.
