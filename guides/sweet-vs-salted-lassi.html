Sweet Lassi vs Salted Lassi: What Is the Difference?

Quick answer: Sweet lassi blends yogurt with sugar and often aromatic flavourings, while salted lassi uses salt and may include cumin or other savoury seasonings.

Sweet lassi

Sweet lassi is creamy and dessert-like, especially when served cold. Fruit versions such as mango lassi add another layer of sweetness.

Salted lassi

Salted or namkeen lassi is savoury and refreshing. Cumin and a light touch of salt are common additions.

Texture tip

Start with cold yogurt and add water or ice gradually so you can control thickness instead of making the drink watery.
