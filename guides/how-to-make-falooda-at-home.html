How to Make Falooda at Home: Layering and Texture Guide

Quick answer: Prepare and chill the milk, soaked basil seeds and noodles separately, then assemble shortly before serving so the drink stays cold and the layers keep their texture.

Prepare each component

Soak basil seeds until fully swollen and cook falooda noodles according to their type. Cool both before assembly.

Keep everything cold

Chilled milk and serving glasses help prevent ice cream from melting too quickly and keep the drink refreshing.

Layer just before serving

Add syrup gradually, then noodles, seeds, milk and ice cream. Adjust sweetness after tasting because syrup and ice cream both contribute sugar.
