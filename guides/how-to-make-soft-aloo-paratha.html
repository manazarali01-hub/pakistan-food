How to Make Soft Aloo Paratha

Quick answer: Use a soft, rested dough and a dry, lump-free potato filling; roll gently and cook on medium heat so the paratha cooks through without becoming hard.

Make a flexible dough

Add water gradually and knead until the dough is smooth and soft, not sticky. Resting relaxes it and makes rolling easier.

Keep filling dry

Watery potatoes can tear the dough. Mash potatoes thoroughly and cool the filling before stuffing.

Cook at moderate heat

A cool pan can dry the paratha before browning, while excessive heat burns the surface. Use medium heat and add fat as needed.
