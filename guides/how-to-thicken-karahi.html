How to Thicken Karahi Without Flour

Quick answer: The best way to thicken Pakistani karahi is usually reduction: cook uncovered so excess water evaporates and the tomato masala concentrates around the meat.

Use a wide pan

A wider cooking surface lets steam escape faster and helps the masala reduce without needing starch.

Cook uncovered near the end

Once the meat is cooked, raise heat moderately and stir as moisture evaporates. Watch closely so the masala does not catch.

Avoid unnecessary thickeners

Flour or cornflour can mute the fresh karahi texture. Proper tomato reduction normally provides enough body.
