How to Store Pakistani Sweets Safely

Quick answer: Storage depends on the sweet: milk-based desserts generally need refrigeration, while some fried syrup sweets tolerate short room-temperature holding better; keep everything covered.

Milk desserts need more care

Kheer, ras malai and similar dairy-rich sweets should be cooled and refrigerated rather than left warm for long periods.

Protect texture

Cover sweets so they do not absorb refrigerator odours. Keep crisp items away from excess moisture whenever possible.

Serve sensible portions

Take out only what you plan to serve and return the rest to safe storage. When in doubt, use the more cautious refrigerated option.
