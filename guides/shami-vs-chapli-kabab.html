Shami Kabab vs Chapli Kabab: Key Differences

Quick answer: Shami kabab combines cooked meat with lentils and is ground into a smooth mixture, while chapli kabab uses a coarser raw mince mixture shaped into flat patties.

Why shami is softer

Cooked lentils and meat are ground together, producing a fine mixture that can be shaped after cooling.

Why chapli is coarser

Chapli kabab keeps more texture in the mince and seasonings. The mixture is shaped directly into wider patties before frying.

Best uses

Shami kabab works well for snacks, sandwiches and freezer batches. Chapli kabab is especially good hot with naan, chutney and salad.
