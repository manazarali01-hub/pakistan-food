How to Make Rice Kheer Thicker and Creamier

Quick answer: Thicker kheer comes from slow reduction and properly cooked rice. Simmer gently, stir regularly and allow the milk to reduce instead of adding large amounts of starch.

Cook rice until tender

The rice should soften enough to release some starch into the milk, helping create a naturally creamy texture.

Reduce slowly

Gentle simmering concentrates milk solids and flavour. Stir the bottom and sides frequently to prevent scorching.

Remember it thickens as it cools

Stop slightly before the final cold consistency you want. Kheer usually becomes noticeably thicker after chilling.
