Why Does Chicken Karahi Taste Too Tomatoey?

Quick answer: Chicken karahi is tomato-led, but it should not taste like raw tomato sauce. In the Pakistan Food version, 5 medium ripe tomatoes for 1 kg chicken are cooked uncovered until they collapse and reduce into a masala that clings to the meat. If tomato still dominates, the usual issue is insufficient reduction or undercooked tomato—not necessarily too many tomatoes.

Raw tomato and concentrated tomato taste very different

Fresh chopped tomato begins watery, acidic and bright. As it cooks, water evaporates, the flesh breaks down and the flavour becomes rounder. Stop too early and the gravy tastes sharp and tomato-forward. Continue controlled reduction and it becomes the characteristic karahi masala.

Use a wide pan and leave it uncovered

A wide karahi gives moisture somewhere to escape. Covering the pan for most of the cook traps steam, leaves tomato water behind and turns the dish into a looser curry. Stir and press the tomatoes as they soften, then reduce until the masala visibly coats the chicken.

Do not add water automatically

Chicken and five tomatoes release substantial moisture. Add only a small splash if the masala is catching before the chicken is cooked. Extra water delays concentration and can force you to cook the chicken too long while trying to repair the sauce.

Finish with the flavours that define karahi

Near the end, add slit green chilli and fresh julienned ginger so they stay aromatic. Fresh coriander goes on at serving. These finishing flavours do not hide raw tomato; they work after the tomato has already been reduced properly.

If the karahi is already too tomato-forward

  • Cook uncovered over medium to medium-high heat and stir more often as it thickens.
  • Check salt after reduction; under-seasoned tomato can taste unusually sharp.
  • Do not add sugar automatically. Sweetness can make the dish taste like tomato sauce rather than karahi.
  • If chicken is already tender, transfer to a wider pan or temporarily lift the chicken out while reducing excess tomato liquid efficiently, then return it.

Roman Urdu

Karahi mein tomato main flavour hota hai lekin raw tomato sauce jaisa taste nahin ana chahye. 1 kg chicken ke liye 5 medium ripe tomatoes ko uncovered reduce karein jab tak masala chicken ko coat na kare. Extra pani na dalen aur end par fresh ginger aur green chilli add karein.
