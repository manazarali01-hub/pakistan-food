How to Stop Yogurt from Splitting in Pakistani Curry

Quick answer: Use room-temperature yogurt, whisk it smooth, lower the heat before adding it, and stir steadily until it is incorporated into the masala.

Temperature matters

Very cold yogurt added to a very hot pan is more likely to separate. Let it lose its chill and reduce the heat first.

Whisk before adding

Smooth yogurt disperses more evenly than yogurt added in lumps. Full-fat yogurt is also generally more stable during cooking.

Cook it into the masala

Stir continuously at first. Once the yogurt is integrated and the masala looks glossy, return to the recipe's normal cooking heat.
