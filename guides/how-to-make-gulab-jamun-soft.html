How to Make Gulab Jamun Soft, Not Hard

Quick answer: Keep the dough soft, shape without cracks, fry slowly at moderate heat and soak the warm jamuns in warm—not aggressively boiling—syrup.

Do not overwork the dough

Heavy kneading can make the balls dense. Bring the mixture together gently and add moisture carefully.

Fry slowly

If the oil is too hot, the outside darkens before the centre cooks. Moderate heat gives the balls time to expand and cook evenly.

Match syrup temperature

Warm syrup absorbs well without shocking the fried jamuns. Give them enough soaking time before judging the final texture.
