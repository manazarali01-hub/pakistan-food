Why Doesn't Gulab Jamun Absorb Syrup?

Quick answer: Control ingredient ratio, heat, moisture and cooling to protect dessert texture.

Start with the ratio

Measure the main ingredients consistently before adjusting technique.

Control heat

Use moderate heat and stir more often as mixtures thicken.

Watch moisture

Reduction continues to change texture, and many desserts thicken further while cooling.

Manage sweetness

Add sugar or syrup with the final concentration in mind.

Use texture cues

Stop at the stage appropriate for serving temperature rather than relying only on time.

Cool correctly

Allow the dessert to settle without exposing it to unnecessary drying.
