How to Keep Ras Malai Soft and Spongy

Quick answer: Soft ras malai depends on moist, smooth chenna, gentle shaping and correct cooking; squeezing out too much moisture or over-kneading can make the discs firm.

Keep the chenna moist enough to shape

Drain fresh curds until they are no longer dripping but still feel supple. Chenna that has been squeezed bone-dry can produce firm discs, while excessively wet chenna can be difficult to shape and may break during cooking.

Knead only until smooth

Work the chenna until obvious graininess reduces and it can form a smooth piece. The goal is a cohesive texture, not prolonged heavy kneading.

Shape even, crack-free discs

Uneven pieces cook at different rates. Smooth the surface and keep the discs similar in size so expansion and cooking are more predictable.

Give the discs room while cooking

Ras malai pieces expand. Use a vessel with enough space and follow your recipe's syrup or cooking-liquid method so the discs can cook without being tightly crowded.

Transfer gently to the flavoured milk

Once cooked, handle the discs carefully. The reduced, flavoured milk should be cool enough for the method you are using and have enough liquid for the pieces to absorb flavour without drying.

Why ras malai turns hard

Over-drained chenna, rough shaping, incorrect cooking conditions or overcooking can all contribute. Correct the chenna and cooking stage first; extra milk cannot fully reverse a dense internal structure.
