How to Keep Ras Malai Soft and Spongy

Quick answer: Soft ras malai depends on moist, smooth chenna, gentle shaping and correct cooking; squeezing out too much moisture or over-kneading can make the discs firm.

Prepare chenna carefully

Drain until the curds are workable but not bone-dry. The moisture level affects whether the discs crack or stay tender.

Shape without cracks

Knead only until smooth enough to shape. Make even discs so they cook at the same rate.

Soak in milk after cooking

After the discs are cooked, let them absorb the flavoured milk. Chilling then improves the finished dessert.
