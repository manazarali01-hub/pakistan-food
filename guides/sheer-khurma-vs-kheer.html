Sheer Khurma vs Kheer: What Is the Difference?

Quick answer: Sheer khurma is a festive vermicelli-and-milk dessert commonly enriched with dates and nuts, while rice kheer is thickened primarily with rice slowly cooked in milk.

The starch is different

Kheer relies on rice starch for body. Sheer khurma uses fine vermicelli, so its texture and cooking time are different.

Typical additions

Dates are strongly associated with sheer khurma, especially for Eid. Kheer commonly highlights cardamom, nuts and reduced milk.

When they are served

Both are traditional milk desserts, but sheer khurma has a strong Eid association, while kheer is served across many celebrations and meals.
