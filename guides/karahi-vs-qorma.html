Karahi vs Qorma: What Is the Difference?

Quick answer: Karahi is typically a brighter, reduced tomato-forward dish cooked relatively quickly, while qorma is usually a deeper gravy built with browned onions, yogurt and warm spices.

Karahi is built around reduction

Karahi commonly uses tomato, meat and spices cooked in a broad pan until excess moisture evaporates and the masala clings to the meat. Fresh ginger, green chilli and coriander are common finishing flavours.

Qorma builds a deeper gravy

Many Pakistani qorma methods develop colour and body from browned onions, then combine yogurt and aromatic spices into a richer sauce. The gravy is generally more deliberate than karahi's reduced masala.

The role of tomato is different

Tomato is central to many karahi recipes. Qorma traditions vary, but browned onion and yogurt are usually more defining than a fresh tomato-forward profile.

The cooking vessels reflect the techniques

A wide karahi encourages evaporation and bhunai. Qorma can be cooked in a deeper pot because retaining and developing gravy is part of the goal.

Both dishes have regional and household variation

No single restaurant version defines either category. Ingredient proportions and spice levels vary, so compare the overall technique rather than deciding from one ingredient alone.

Which one should you make?

Choose karahi for a brighter, reduced tomato masala with fresh finishing aromatics. Choose qorma for a deeper browned-onion and yogurt gravy with warm spices.
