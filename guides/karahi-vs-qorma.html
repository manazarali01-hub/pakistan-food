Karahi vs Qorma: What Is the Difference?

Quick answer: Karahi is typically a brighter, reduced tomato-forward dish cooked relatively quickly, while qorma is usually a deeper gravy built with browned onions, yogurt and warm spices.

Karahi cooking style

Karahi relies on high-heat reduction and a concentrated masala. Fresh ginger, green chilli and coriander are common finishing flavours.

Qorma cooking style

Qorma commonly develops body from browned onions and yogurt, with a richer gravy and aromatic whole or ground spices.

Which recipe to choose

Choose karahi when you want a fresher, reduced masala; choose qorma when you want a more gravy-based, aromatic curry.
