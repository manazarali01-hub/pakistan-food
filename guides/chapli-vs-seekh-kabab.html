Chapli Kabab vs Seekh Kabab: What Is the Difference?

Quick answer: Chapli kabab is a flat, pan-fried minced-meat patty with a coarse seasoned texture; seekh kabab is shaped around skewers and cooked over direct heat or a grill.

Shape and texture

Chapli kabab is broad and flat, while seekh kabab is long and cylindrical. Shape changes how quickly each cooks and browns.

Seasoning style

Chapli kabab often includes visible herbs and textured additions. Seekh mixtures are usually worked more thoroughly so they hold to the skewer.

Cooking method

Chapli kabab is commonly shallow-fried. Seekh kabab is designed for skewers, though it can also be cooked in an oven or grill pan.
