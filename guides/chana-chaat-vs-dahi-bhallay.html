Chana Chaat vs Dahi Bhallay: What Is the Difference?

Quick answer: Chana chaat is chickpea-focused and tangy, while dahi bhallay centres on soft lentil dumplings covered with yogurt, chutneys and spices.

Main ingredients

Chana chaat starts with cooked chickpeas and often includes potato and chutney. Dahi bhallay starts with fried lentil dumplings softened before serving.

Texture

Chana chaat has more bite from chickpeas and vegetables. Dahi bhallay is intentionally soft and creamy because the dumplings absorb yogurt.

Serving style

Both are popular snack and Ramadan foods. Assemble close to serving time so garnishes stay fresh and flavours remain distinct.
