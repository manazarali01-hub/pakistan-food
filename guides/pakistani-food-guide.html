Pakistani Food Guide: Essential Dishes, Flavours and Cooking Styles

Quick answer: Pakistani food includes rice dishes, slow-cooked meat curries, karahi-style dishes, barbecue, lentils, vegetables, breads, snacks, sweets and drinks. Regional and household variations are a major part of the cuisine.

What matters most

Biryani and pulao showcase different rice techniques; nihari and haleem use long cooking; karahi emphasizes a concentrated tomato-spice masala; seekh kabab and tikka highlight grilling and barbecue.

Technique and common mistakes

Everyday meals can be much simpler than restaurant menus: daal, vegetables, rice and flatbreads are important parts of home cooking alongside richer celebration dishes.

Practical tip

Use this guide as a starting point, then follow individual recipes for measured ingredients and step-by-step methods.
