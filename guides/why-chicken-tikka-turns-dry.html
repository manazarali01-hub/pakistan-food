Why Does Chicken Tikka Turn Dry? 6 Mistakes to Avoid

Quick answer: Chicken tikka becomes dry mainly when lean pieces lose too much moisture through excessive cooking. Even sizing, appropriate marination and stopping once the chicken is safely cooked help protect texture.

1. Cut pieces to a similar thickness

Use similarly sized boneless cubes or evenly portioned bone-in pieces. Thin edges cook faster than thick centres, so mixed sizes often leave some pieces dry while others are still underdone. Breast meat has less fat than thigh meat, so it needs especially careful timing.

2. Keep the marinade balanced

Yogurt helps the marinade cling and adds tang, while ginger-garlic paste and spices build flavour. Too much lemon juice or vinegar for too long can affect the outer texture; follow a tested recipe and refrigerate chicken while marinating. The marinade should coat the pieces rather than leave them swimming in liquid.

3. Shake off excess marinade before cooking

Let excess marinade drip back into the bowl before the chicken goes onto a hot grill, pan or oven tray. A thick wet coating can steam the surface instead of browning it. Lightly oil the cooking surface if needed to reduce sticking rather than repeatedly brushing on large amounts of oil.

4. Use heat that browns without burning

Preheat the grill, oven or pan. Turn pieces as needed so they colour evenly; if the outside chars long before the centre cooks, reduce the heat or move the pieces farther from direct flame. Small cubes need less time than large pieces, so do not copy timings blindly between cuts or appliances.

5. Check doneness safely, not by colour alone

Charred edges do not prove the centre is cooked, and colour alone is not a reliable thermometer. Check the thickest piece with a food thermometer: chicken should reach 74°C (165°F). Remove it once safely cooked rather than leaving it on the heat “just in case.”

6. Rest briefly and serve promptly

Let tikka rest for a few minutes after cooking so juices can settle, then serve. If cooking in batches, keep finished pieces warm without continuing to cook them over direct heat. Refrigerate leftovers promptly; repeated reheating can make lean chicken drier.

Roman Urdu: Chicken tikka dry kyun ho jata hai?

Roman Urdu: Chicken tikka aksar overcooking, bohat chote ya uneven pieces, ya uncontrolled high heat ki wajah se dry hota hai. Pieces even rakhein, marinade ko balance karein aur chicken ko zaroorat se zyada na pakayein.
