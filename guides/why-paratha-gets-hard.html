Why Does Paratha Get Hard? 6 Mistakes That Ruin Softness

Quick answer: Paratha often turns hard when the dough is too dry, has not rested enough, is rolled with excessive dry flour, or stays on the tawa too long. Fix the dough and cooking process before simply adding more oil.

1. Check the ingredient balance first

Many cooking failures start before heat is applied. Ingredient proportions affect moisture, structure and flavour, so correct the base rather than relying on a last-minute rescue.

2. Preparation changes the final texture

Resting, draining, mixing and shaping are not minor details. Skipping them can create a problem that becomes obvious only during cooking.

3. Heat that is too high can create a false shortcut

High heat may colour the outside quickly while leaving the inside texture behind. Use heat appropriate to the food and adjust it as cooking progresses.

4. Too much cooking can be as damaging as too little

Once the desired structure has developed, extra time can remove moisture or make the texture tougher. Watch the food itself instead of relying only on a timer.

5. Make one correction at a time

If a batch goes wrong, change one major variable on the next attempt. This makes it easier to identify the actual cause and avoids over-correcting.

6. Judge the process, not just the final appearance

Texture, aroma and resistance during cooking provide useful clues. Recognizing them early lets you correct the process before the final dish is compromised.

Roman Urdu: Paratha hard kyun ho jata hai?

Roman Urdu: Paratha aksar dry dough, kam resting, belte waqt bohat zyada dry atta, ya taway par zyada der pakane ki wajah se hard hota hai. Sirf extra oil dalne ke bajaye dough aur cooking time theek karein.
