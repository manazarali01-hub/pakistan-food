How to Make Gajar ka Halwa Without Burning

Quick answer: Use a heavy pan, moderate heat and increasingly frequent stirring as the milk reduces; add sugar at the right stage and scrape the base clean with each stir.

Use a broad heavy pan

A sturdy pan spreads heat more evenly. A broad surface also speeds evaporation without requiring extreme heat.

Stir more as it thickens

Early in cooking the mixture contains plenty of liquid. As moisture evaporates, the risk of catching on the base rises sharply.

Reduce patiently

High heat can scorch milk solids before the carrots are ready. Moderate heat gives a cleaner flavour and better control over the final texture.
