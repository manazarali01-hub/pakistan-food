How to Cook Onions for Pakistani Qorma

Quick answer: For a 1 kg chicken qorma using about 3 medium thinly sliced onions, fry the onions gradually until evenly deep golden, not dark brown or black. Remove and drain them, let them cool slightly, then crush them before returning most to the masala. The onion should thicken and sweeten the gravy without tasting burnt.

Why the onion stage matters so much

Qorma does not get its characteristic body from tomato reduction like karahi. Fried onion, yogurt, fat and spices form the gravy. If the onions are pale, the qorma can taste raw and thin. If they are burnt, the bitterness spreads through the whole pot and becomes harder to hide as the gravy reduces.

Slice evenly before the oil is hot

Uneven onion slices create two problems at once: thin edges burn while thick pieces remain pale. Slice the onions as evenly as practical, separate the layers and use a heavy pot with enough surface area that they are not piled in a wet mass.

Use colour, not a fixed minute count

Onion stageWhat it means for qorma
Pale and softNot enough roasted sweetness or body; the gravy may taste onion-raw.
Even medium-to-deep goldenBest target: aromatic, sweet-savory and easy to crush into the gravy.
Very dark brown/black edgesBitterness risk. Burnt pieces should not be mixed back into the qorma.

Remember carryover browning

Onions continue darkening briefly after they leave the oil. Remove them just before they look “perfect” if your oil is still very hot. Spread them rather than leaving a hot dense pile, because trapped heat keeps cooking the centre.

Why crush the fried onion?

Once cool enough to handle, crushed onion disperses into the yogurt masala more evenly than large fried strands. In the Pakistan Food chicken qorma method, most of the crushed onion returns after the yogurt and spices are added; a little can be reserved for garnish.

How onions interact with yogurt

Whisk the yogurt smooth before adding it, lower the heat and stir it in gradually. Once the yogurt has cooked past its raw tang, the crushed onion helps the gravy become cohesive. Bhunai at this stage should smell rounded and aromatic, not sharply sour or burnt.

Common onion mistakes

  • Using high heat to rush browning and burning thin edges.
  • Returning onions while still very oily and hot, which can make the gravy feel greasy.
  • Using heavily burnt onion because “qorma is supposed to be dark.” Dark gravy should come from controlled browning, not char.
  • Leaving large crisp onion strands in the gravy when you want a smooth qorma body.

Qorma ke liye piyaz ko evenly slice karein aur medium heat par deep golden karein. Black ya burnt edges qorma ko karwa kar sakti hain. Fried piyaz ko nikaal kar thora cool karein, crush karein aur yogurt masalay mein wapas add karein.
