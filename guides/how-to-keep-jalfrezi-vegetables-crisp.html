How to Keep Jalfrezi Vegetables Crisp

Quick answer: Cook the chicken first, add vegetables late, and use fairly high heat so peppers and onions stay bright and lightly crisp instead of steaming until soft.

Cook the chicken before the quick-cooking vegetables

Chicken releases moisture while it cooks. Let it cook through according to the recipe and allow excess liquid to reduce before adding bell peppers and onions, otherwise the vegetables are more likely to simmer in steam.

Use a wide, well-heated pan

A broad hot cooking surface lets moisture escape quickly and gives ingredients room to contact the pan. Overcrowding lowers the temperature and turns a stir-fry-style finish into a wetter braise.

Cut vegetables into even pieces

Similar-size pepper and onion pieces cook at a similar rate. Very small pieces soften before larger pieces lose their raw edge, making it difficult to keep a consistent bite.

Add peppers and onions near the end

They need far less cooking than chicken. Add them once the sauce and protein are nearly ready, then cook just long enough to take off the raw edge while preserving colour and texture.

Do not cover the pan at the final stage

A lid traps steam around the vegetables. Unless your specific recipe calls for it, keep the final high-heat vegetable stage uncovered so released moisture can escape.

If your jalfrezi is already watery

Remove or hold back delicate vegetables if practical, then reduce excess sauce over stronger heat before combining. Continuing to boil everything together may thicken the sauce but can leave the peppers and onions limp.
