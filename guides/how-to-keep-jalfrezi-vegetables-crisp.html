How to Keep Jalfrezi Vegetables Crisp

Quick answer: Cook the chicken first, add vegetables late, and use fairly high heat so peppers and onions stay bright and lightly crisp instead of steaming until soft.

Cook the protein first

Chicken releases moisture as it cooks. Let excess liquid reduce before adding the vegetables so they are stir-fried rather than boiled.

Add vegetables near the end

Bell peppers and onions need only a short cook. Cut them evenly and add them once the chicken and sauce are nearly finished.

Avoid crowding the pan

A wide hot pan lets moisture escape quickly. If the pan is overcrowded, the temperature drops and the vegetables soften.
