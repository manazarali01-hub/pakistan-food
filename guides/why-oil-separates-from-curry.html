Why Does Oil Separate From Curry?

Quick answer: The result depends on the balance of the curry base, moisture, heat and final seasoning.

Check the base

Cook onions, tomatoes and aromatics to the intended stage so raw or burnt notes do not dominate.

Control moisture

Reduce excess liquid gradually and lower heat as the masala becomes concentrated.

Balance seasoning

Salt, chilli, acidity and fat interact, so adjust one element at a time.

Watch aroma

Aroma is a useful cue for the transition from raw ingredients to a developed masala.

Protect texture

Avoid unnecessary cooking after meat or vegetables reach the desired tenderness.

Finish carefully

Use fresh garnishes and final seasoning to complement, not hide, the cooked base.

Roman Urdu: Salan se oil alag kyun hota hai?

Masala, moisture aur heat ko balance karke stage by stage cook karein.
