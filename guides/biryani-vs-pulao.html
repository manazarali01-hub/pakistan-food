Biryani vs Pulao: What Is the Difference?

Quick answer: Biryani and pulao are both rice dishes, but biryani commonly uses separately par-cooked rice layered with a seasoned meat or vegetable mixture, while pulao more often cooks rice in a seasoned stock with the main ingredients.

What matters most

Biryani usually builds flavour in stages: masala, parboiled rice, layering and dum. Pulao commonly develops flavour in the stock and finishes as a one-pot rice preparation.

Technique and common mistakes

There are many regional versions, so no single rule covers every household. The useful distinction is cooking method rather than simply how spicy the dish tastes.

Practical tip

Try both methods to see the contrast in texture and flavour: biryani emphasizes layers, while pulao emphasizes rice cooked in seasoned broth.
