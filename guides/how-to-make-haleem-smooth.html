How to Make Haleem Smooth and Creamy

Quick answer: Smooth haleem comes from fully tender grains and meat, patient cooking, and thorough mixing or mashing before the final tempering.

Cook grains until very soft

Wheat and lentils need enough water and time to lose their firm centres. Soaking first can shorten cooking time and helps them break down evenly.

Shred and blend gradually

Tender meat should shred easily. Combine it with the cooked grains, then mash or blend in stages rather than trying to fix a coarse texture at the end.

Control the final thickness

Haleem thickens as it rests. Keep it slightly looser than your final target while hot, then finish with fried onions, ginger and lemon.
