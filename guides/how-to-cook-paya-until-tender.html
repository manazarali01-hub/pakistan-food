How to Cook Paya Until Tender

Quick answer: Paya needs slow, moist cooking until connective tissue softens and the broth becomes naturally rich; a pressure cooker can shorten the process.

Why paya takes time

Trotters contain collagen-rich connective tissue. Gentle cooking converts it into gelatin, giving paya its characteristic body.

Pressure cooker or pot

A pressure cooker is faster, while a heavy pot gives more control over reduction. In either case, do not let the liquid run dry.

Finish the broth properly

Once tender, simmer uncovered if the broth is too thin. Adjust salt and spices after the texture is close to finished.
