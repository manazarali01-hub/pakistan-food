How Long Should Chicken Tikka Marinate?

Quick answer: Chicken tikka benefits from enough marinating time for seasoning to coat and flavour the meat; a few hours in the refrigerator is practical, while an overnight marinade can be used when the recipe is balanced.

A few refrigerated hours is a practical target

Chicken tikka benefits from enough time for yogurt, spices and salt to coat the pieces thoroughly. A few hours in the refrigerator works well for many recipes, while a balanced marinade can often be prepared the night before.

More time is not automatically better

Very acidic marinades can change the outer texture of chicken when left for a long period. Follow the recipe's lemon, vinegar or other acidic quantity rather than adding extra in the hope of forcing flavour deeper.

Keep marinating chicken refrigerated

Use a covered, food-safe non-reactive container in the refrigerator. Do not leave raw chicken marinating on the kitchen counter for hours.

Cut pieces evenly before marinating

Similar-size pieces cook at similar rates and receive a more even coating of marinade. Very small pieces can dry before large pieces are safely cooked through.

Remove only excessive surface marinade before high heat

The coating provides flavour, but large wet clumps can inhibit browning or drip heavily under a grill. Follow your recipe's cooking method and arrange pieces with space for heat circulation.

Cook for doneness, not char alone

Dark edges are part of tikka's appeal, but exterior colour does not prove the centre is safely cooked. Use proper doneness checks and avoid extending cooking unnecessarily once the chicken is ready.
