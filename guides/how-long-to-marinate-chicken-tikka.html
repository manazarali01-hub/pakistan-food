How Long Should Chicken Tikka Marinate?

Quick answer: Chicken tikka benefits from enough marinating time for seasoning to coat and flavour the meat; a few hours in the refrigerator is practical, while an overnight marinade can be used when the recipe is balanced.

Keep it refrigerated

Always marinate chicken in the refrigerator, not on the counter. Use a covered non-reactive container.

Balance acidic ingredients

Too much lemon or other acid for a very long time can change the outer texture. Follow a balanced yogurt-and-spice marinade rather than adding extra acid.

Cook by doneness, not colour alone

Charred edges are desirable, but the centre still needs to be safely cooked. Avoid drying small pieces with excessive cooking.
