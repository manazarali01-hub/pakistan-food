Traditional Lassi vs Mango Lassi

Quick answer: Traditional lassi highlights yogurt and may be sweet or salted, while mango lassi blends yogurt with mango for a thicker, fruit-forward drink.

The common base

Both drinks rely on yogurt, but the yogurt itself changes the result. Thick full-fat yogurt gives more body, while a tart yogurt gives a sharper flavour. Whisk or blend the yogurt smooth before adding other ingredients so the finished drink is creamy rather than grainy.

How mango changes texture and sweetness

Mango contributes fruit solids as well as sugar, so mango lassi is usually thicker and often needs less added sugar than plain sweet lassi. Very ripe mango can make the drink almost spoonable; loosen it with cold water or milk a little at a time rather than diluting it all at once.

Traditional lassi can be sweet or salted

Plain lassi is not one fixed recipe. Sweet versions may use sugar and cardamom, while salted versions can include salt and savoury seasonings. Mango lassi is a separate fruit-forward variation rather than a replacement for these traditional styles.

How to choose between them

Choose plain lassi when you want the yogurt flavour to lead or when serving a rich, spicy meal. Choose mango lassi when ripe mango is available and you want a sweeter, fuller drink that can work almost like a light dessert.

Adjust before serving

Yogurt tartness and mango sweetness vary from batch to batch. Blend first, chill, taste, then adjust sugar and dilution. Remember that a very cold drink can taste less sweet than the same mixture at room temperature.
