Traditional Lassi vs Mango Lassi

Quick answer: Traditional lassi highlights yogurt and may be sweet or salted, while mango lassi blends yogurt with mango for a thicker, fruit-forward drink.

The common base

Both drinks rely on yogurt and benefit from a smooth, well-chilled base.

How mango changes texture

Mango adds sweetness, flavour and body, so mango lassi often needs less added sugar and may be thicker.

Adjust before serving

Yogurt tartness and mango sweetness vary. Blend first, taste, then adjust sugar or water instead of using a fixed amount blindly.
