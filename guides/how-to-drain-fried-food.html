How to Drain Fried Food Without Making It Soggy

Quick answer: Learn how racks, brief draining and ventilation help pakoras and samosas stay crisper after frying.

Let excess oil leave immediately

This is the first variable to check because it directly changes the moisture, structure or flavour of the dish.

Use a rack when crispness matters

Control this factor before adding extra ingredients. Correcting the cause is more reliable than masking the result later.

Avoid stacking very hot snacks

Watch the food as it cooks. Texture, aroma and visible moisture usually show whether this stage is progressing correctly.

Do not seal in trapped steam

Keep adjustments small and deliberate so you can tell which change actually improved the result.

Paper can help briefly but needs airflow

Home cookware and ingredient moisture vary, so use the stated principle rather than forcing one exact time on every batch.

Serve or cool with ventilation

Once this factor is under control, finish the dish normally and note what worked for the next batch.
