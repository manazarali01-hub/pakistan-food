How to Temper Daal for Better Aroma

Quick answer: Ingredient condition, moisture, heat and timing determine the result.

Check preparation

Start with properly prepared ingredients and a consistent ratio.

Manage water

Add liquid deliberately and account for absorption during cooking.

Control heat

Use steady heat and reduce it as the mixture thickens.

Watch texture

Judge doneness by tenderness and consistency as well as time.

Season in stages

Adjust salt, spice and acidity gradually.

Finish carefully

Stop when the intended texture is reached and avoid unnecessary cooking.

Roman Urdu: Daal ko tarka kaise lagayein?

Preparation, pani, heat aur seasoning ko balance karein.
