How to Cook Chickpeas Until Tender

Quick answer: Soak dried chickpeas well, cook them in fresh water until completely tender, and add strongly acidic ingredients after they have softened for a more predictable texture.

Soaking helps

An overnight soak usually shortens cooking and promotes more even tenderness. Discard the soaking water and rinse before cooking.

Cook before judging the curry

Chickpeas should be tender inside before the final masala is reduced. Old dried chickpeas can take considerably longer than fresh stock.

Finish with the masala

Once tender, simmer the chickpeas with the spiced onion-tomato base so they absorb flavour without relying on prolonged hard boiling.
