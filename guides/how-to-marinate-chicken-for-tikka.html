How to Marinate Chicken for Tikka

Quick answer: Control mixture moisture, seasoning, shaping and cooking heat for a reliable BBQ or kabab result.

Prepare the mixture

Use evenly cut or mixed ingredients and remove unnecessary surface moisture.

Balance the marinade

Spices, yogurt, acid and salt should coat the food rather than form a watery layer.

Control temperature

Use enough heat for browning while avoiding prolonged exposure that dries the food.

Handle gently

Let the surface set before excessive turning or moving.

Check doneness

Cook meat thoroughly while avoiding unnecessary extra cooking after it is done.

Rest and serve

A brief rest can help the cooked food settle before serving.

Roman Urdu

Marinade, moisture, shaping aur heat ko balance karein taa ke BBQ ya kabab juicy aur stable rahe.

میرینیڈ، نمی، شکل اور آنچ کو متوازن رکھیں۔
