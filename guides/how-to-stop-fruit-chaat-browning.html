How to Stop Fruit Chaat from Browning

Quick answer: Cut browning-prone fruit close to serving time and coat it promptly with citrus-rich dressing; keep the prepared chaat covered and chilled.

Cut delicate fruit last

Apples and bananas discolor quickly after cutting. Prepare firmer fruits first and leave these until near the end.

Use citrus promptly

Lemon or orange juice lowers surface pH and can slow enzymatic browning while also adding the tang expected in fruit chaat.

Chill, but do not store too long

Refrigeration helps, but fruit releases juice and softens over time. Fruit chaat has its best texture when assembled reasonably close to serving.
