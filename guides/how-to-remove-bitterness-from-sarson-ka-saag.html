How to Balance Bitterness in Sarson ka Saag

Quick answer: Mustard greens naturally have a pleasant bitterness; balance it with other greens, proper cooking, salt and a rich finishing tarka rather than removing every bitter note.

Mixing greens helps

Spinach or bathua can soften mustard greens' sharp edge while keeping the characteristic saag flavour.

Cook long enough

Raw mustard greens can taste harsh. Slow cooking mellows them and develops a deeper flavour.

Finish for balance

Ghee, garlic, chilli and cornmeal can round out the final dish. Taste before making large seasoning changes.
