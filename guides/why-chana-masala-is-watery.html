Why Is Chana Masala Watery?

Quick answer: Consistent preparation, moisture control, moderate heat and staged seasoning give the most reliable result.

Prepare consistently

Start with clean, evenly prepared ingredients so they cook at a similar rate.

Control the liquid

Account for absorption and evaporation instead of adding water automatically.

Use responsive heat

Adjust the flame as the mixture softens and thickens.

Check texture

Tenderness and consistency are better finishing cues than time alone.

Season gradually

Add salt, spice and acidity in stages and taste after the base has developed.

Finish deliberately

Stop at the intended texture and add finishing aromatics without overcooking them.

Roman Urdu
